## 🧪 SurgXBench: Explainable Vision-Language Model Benchmark for Surgery  
**WACV 2026 (Accepted)**  
*Jiajun Cheng, Xianwu Zhao, Sainan Liu, Xiaofan Yu, Ravi Prakash, Patrick Codd, Jonathan Katz, Shan Lin*

SurgXBench introduces the first explainability-driven benchmark for Vision-Language Models in robotic surgery.
We evaluate general & surgical VLMs for instrument and action recognition, visualize model attention using Grad-CAM
and causal XAI graphs, and propose new attention-alignment metrics to measure whether models rely on clinically
meaningful cues. Results reveal a reasoning–accuracy gap, motivating stronger visual grounding in future surgical VLMs.

📄 **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10764  
💻 **Code:** https://github.com/jiajun344/SurgXBench-Explainable-Vision-Language-Model-Benchmark-for-Surgery
