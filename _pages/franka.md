### <span style="color: #8C1D40;">Key Features</span>

- Dual-arm collaborative robotic platform
- Integrated joint torque sensing
- High-precision impedance and force control
- Dual 7-DoF robotic manipulators

---

### <span style="color: #8C1D40;">Projects</span>

- Bimanual manipulation research
- Vision-language-action (VLA) model development
- Robot learning and imitation learning
- Human-robot collaboration studies
