<div style="border-top: 3px solid #8C1D40; width: 60px; margin-bottom: 20px;"></div>
<p style="color: #444; font-size: 1em; line-height: 1.6;">The Phantom Touch is a force-feedback haptic device that enables teleoperation and human-in-the-loop interaction with robotic systems.</p>

---

### <span style="color: #8C1D40;">Key Features</span>

- 6-DoF position sensing
- 3-DoF force feedback
- 1 kHz haptic servo loop

---

### <span style="color: #8C1D40;">Projects</span>

- Surgical teleoperation research
- Shared autonomy studies
- Human–robot interaction research
- Skill acquisition and training experiments
